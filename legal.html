Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy outlines how Ashley Harrington Fine Art collects, processes, protects, and discloses information from collectors, visitors, and users of our online website and active studio platforms. Your trust is crucial to our creative practice.

Information Collection & Usage

We only collect personal credentials that you directly cooperate with, including names, billing/shipping destinations, telephone coordinates, and contact email addresses when purchasing paintings through our catalog or requesting commission information.

  • Studio Mailing Newsletter: Subscribers store contact email profiles safely to receive seasonal collection previews. You can safely unsubscribe instantly.
  • Digital Cookies & Pixels: We utilize light web tracking indicators to analyze device traffic variables, screen dimensions, and browsing speeds to improve interface design.
  • Payment Processing Integration: Transaction parameters are handled directly via payment service partners (Stripe & PayPal) with high-grade banking firewalls. The artist never retains financial credentials.

Online Studio Shop & Transactions

Artworks are offered subject to availability. While we make every attempt to render the physical color, depth, and fine plaster textures of the canvases as accurately as possible, individual screen calibration scales vary. Minor visual differences are inherent to hand-crafted art.
