Shipping & Delivery
Original canvases are wrapped in heavy museum-grade acid-free glassine paper, surrounded by thick shock-absorbent foam inserts, and placed in heavy-duty wooden crates built specifically for each painting size. This ensures zero pressure is applied directly to the custom-textured oil paint skin. All original paintings are shipped with signature confirmation and full insurance.
Yes, worldwide courier shipping is fully supported. Standard architectural sheets and heavy stock giclée documents are lightly rolled with protective tissue paper and dispatched in extra-thick heavy cardboard mailing tubes. Shipping fees and custom duty charges are calculated automatically at checkout based on destination region.
Handling of prints takes 2–4 business days as they are custom manufactured on demand. Standard transit times are 3–7 business days within the USA and 7–14 days for international orders. For original art, crate preparation and courier scheduling require 7–10 days prior to dispatch. Tracking codes are emailed immediately upon courier collection.
Originals & Custom Requests
The original canvases are constructed by building tactile layered structures using oil mediums, premium plasters, dense volcanic dusts, and raw natural earth pigments. Everything is manually applied to heavy 100% organic Belgian linen blocks stretched over high-durability spruce wood finger-joint frames.
Ashley accepts a limited number of custom commissions each season depending on ongoing exhibition commitments. Commissions require a 50% deposit with a minimum frame coordinate of 24" x 24". To explore a collaboration, please fill out the specialized questionnaire form on the contact page.
Private viewings of current active inventory at the Massachusetts private workspace can be arranged exclusively with prior scheduling. Sessions are intended for collectors, curators, and institutional gallery partners. Please submit an email request via the contact page to reserve a time.
Prints & Professional Framing
Fine art prints hosted on the shop page are giclée reproductions on heavy velvet archival paper. Standard open-edition prints do not feature individual artist markings. Exclusive limited-collection series (offered during specific seasonal cycles) are hand-signed and sequentially numbered by the artist and arrive shipped with a formal Certificate of Authenticity.
All fine art prints are currently dispatched unframed, wrapped, and prepared globally for dynamic shipping. This allows collectors to pair each piece with custom moldings, museum frames, or mat borders that specifically match their local interior environments. Standard size parameters are designed to integrate seamlessly into widely available off-the-shelf frame profiles.
Returns & Security Policy
Due to the unique nature of print-on-demand giclées and high-value originals, all paint and print transactions are final. If an item arrives with any postal transit defects or transit damages, please document the carton's outer wear and canvas layers, and contact the studio within 48 hours. Secure replacement prints or insured claims will be processed immediately.
Our shop features fully-integrated end-to-end security encryption. All payment integrations are processed using external banking networks (Stripe and PayPal) that protect customer data behind advanced firewalls and standard TLS verification protocols. The studio never stores or records collector card credentials.
