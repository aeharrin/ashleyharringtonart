The Artist

Ashley Harrington

Based in New England, my practice is split into two visual worlds: the heavy, wind-sculpted shorelines of coastal landscapes, and highly textured, raw physical expressions of figure work using palette knives and earthy raw pigments.

Each piece serves as a registry of time—using generous paint layering, natural clay textures, and warm, sunlit color chords to observe how wind, earth, and physical human form collide.
